## 3. Directly implemented in v0.2

### Candidate and score contracts

- explicit raw/log-likelihood/logit/preference/probability semantics;
- refusal to use uncalibrated preferences as probabilities;
- path-preserving CTranslate2 N-best adapter;
- score-domain-safe logsumexp surface pooling;
- source-support and decoder-path provenance;
- Semantic MBR with surface, mora, critical-token, and preservation losses;
- adaptive K by posterior mass, risk, criticality, and diversity;
- conservative fusion–MBR disagreement gate.

### Learned ranking

- dependency-free pairwise logistic trainer;
- reproducible Japanese hard-negative generator;
- model-free training smoke test that must improve loss and pairwise accuracy;
- optional identity-activation CrossEncoder raw-logit adapter;
- optional Qwen3-Reranker raw yes-vs-no logit adapter;
- explicit held-out calibration hook before ranker evidence enters fusion.

### Sparse and residual computation

- query-selected acoustic verifier with three gated branches;
- branch-balance regularization;
- quantile-balanced evidence-action router;
- empirical reward updates;
- bounded residual branch mixer;
- low-cost to high-cost deployment tiers.

### Teacher efficiency

- keyed hashed next-token probability cache;
- longest-suffix backoff and miss penalty;
- teacher/revision provenance;
- no raw context stored in cache files.

## 5. Hypotheses now testable

1. Decoder path-mass aggregation improves calibration and top-1 selection over strongest-path deduplication.
2. Semantic MBR reduces number/date/currency/negation errors at similar aggregate CER.
3. Adaptive K reaches the same oracle quality with lower mean reranker cost than fixed K.
4. A 130M Japanese CrossEncoder approaches or exceeds a generic 0.6B reranker on CPU latency-quality frontier.
5. An ASR-finetuned Qwen3-Reranker improves rank regret without increasing unsupported correction.
6. Query-selected acoustic verification resolves language-model/acoustic conflicts more cheaply than always invoking a second ASR.
7. Quantile-balanced action routing avoids permanent second-ear starvation under domain shift.
8. Cached teacher probabilities recover part of an 8B/12B teacher gain without online teacher inference.
9. Synthetic hard negatives help critical-token robustness only when mixed with real decoder errors; synthetic-only training will overfit its corruption grammar.
10. Guarded GER can improve beyond N-best oracle only when every generated proposal is acoustically reverified.

None is presented as measured until the named held-out Japanese benchmark is run.

## 7. Training stages

```text
Stage 0  collect immutable ASR N-best and score provenance
Stage 1  construct real candidate losses and semantic slices
Stage 2  train/evaluate dependency-free linear ranker
Stage 3  fine-tune 130M CrossEncoder with pairwise/listwise/MWER objectives
Stage 4  LoRA-fine-tune Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B
Stage 5  train acoustic verifier from frozen speech-encoder features
Stage 6  distil offline 8B/12B/frontier teacher preferences and probabilities
Stage 7  calibrate every changed score path on a speaker-disjoint split
Stage 8  lock test set and run complete ablation
Stage 9  export INT8/INT4 edge variants and rerun calibration
```

A model trained on synthetic corruption alone is never designated production-ready.

## 8. Evidence standard

A unit test proves a contract or deterministic algorithm. Synthetic smoke training proves that the trainer can optimize its own fixtures. Neither proves real speech-recognition improvement.

A quality claim requires:

- exact audio manifest and rights state;
- exact model and runtime revisions;
- speaker/source/near-duplicate-disjoint splits;
- calibration split separate from training and test;
- paired bootstrap confidence intervals;
- aggregate and slice metrics;
- negative examples and regression cases;
- RTF, peak RAM/VRAM, energy or CPU time where available;
- disclosure of second-ear, teacher, verifier, and abstention invocation rates.
